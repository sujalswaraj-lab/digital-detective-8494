# Task 1.3 — Computer systems around you

| Computer-based system | Main purpose | One input | One output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Automated teller machine (ATM) | Provides banking services such as cash withdrawal. | Card and PIN entered by a customer | Cash dispensed |
| Traffic-light control system | Directs vehicles and pedestrians at an intersection. | Vehicle detector or pedestrian crossing button | Traffic signal changes color |
| Supermarket self-checkout | Lets a shopper scan and pay for selected goods. | Barcode scanned from an item | Updated on-screen total |

Each example receives information through a sensor, button, card reader, or scanner; processes it using a computer; and produces a useful physical or on-screen result.
